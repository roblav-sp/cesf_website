Mediumship

From Leon Denis, in the book: In the Invisible

All manifestations of nature and life can be summarized in vibrations, more or less rapid and extensive, according to the causes that produce them. Everything vibrates in the universe: sound, light, heat, electricity, magnetism, chemical rays, cathode rays, radio waves, etc., are only the various modes of undulation, vibration of universal force and substance, the successive degrees that constitute, as a whole, the ascending scale of energy manifestations.

These degrees are very far apart from each other. Sound travels 340 meters per second; light, in the same time, crosses 300,000 kilometers; electricity is propagated with a speed that seems incalculable to us. But our physical senses do not allow us to perceive all modes of vibration. Their inability to give us a complete impression of nature’s forces is a well-known fact that we do not need to insist on further.

Only in the field of optics do we know that light waves only impress our retina within the limits of the seven colors of the prism, from red to purple. Beyond or below these colors, solar radiations escape our sight; they are then called dark rays.

Between the limit of sounds, whose vibrations are 24 to 60,000 per second, and the sensation of heat, which is measured by trillions of vibrations, we perceive nothing. It is the same between the sensation of heat and that of light, which corresponds, on average, to 500 trillion vibrations per second.

In this stupendous ascent, our senses represent widely spaced floors, stations placed at considerable distances from each other on an endless road. Between these various stages, for example, between high-pitched sounds and the phenomena of heat and light, from there to the vibrational zones affected by cathode rays, there are for us as chasms. But would not these chasms, apparently empty and obscure, be filled by beings endowed with senses more subtle or more numerous than ours? Between the vibrations perceived by hearing and those that impress our vision, is there nothing but emptiness in the domain of forces and universal life?

It would make little sense to believe so, because everything in nature follows, is connected and unfolds, from one ring to another, through gradual transitions. Nowhere is there a sudden jump, void, hiatus. What emerges from these considerations is simply the insufficiency of our organism, too poor to perceive all modes of energy.

What we say about the forces in action in the universe also applies to all beings and things, in their various forms, at their various degrees of condensation or rarefaction.

Our knowledge of the universe is restricted or expanded, depending on the number and subtlety of our senses. Our present organism only allows us to embrace a very limited circle of the empire of things. Many forms of life escape us. But let a new sense be added to our senses, and immediately the invisible is revealed, the emptiness is populated, dark insensitivity gains life.
