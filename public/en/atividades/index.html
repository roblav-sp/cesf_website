Public Activities

Study Meetings and Mini-Lectures Open meetings for people of all ages who wish to discover or deepen their knowledge about spiritualism.

Wednesdays (in French): mini-lectures, magnetic passes activity for those who wish and systematic study of spiritualist philosophy.

Time: 7:00 PM to 9:30 PM

Saturdays (in French): systematic study of spiritualist philosophy and magnetic passes for those who desire them.

Time: 11:00 AM to 12:45 PM

Workshop for Children and Young People (in French and Portuguese) Meetings / activities scheduled for children and young people (3 to 13 years old), guided according to the moral teachings of spiritualist philosophy. These meetings take place on Saturdays, every other week, from 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM. In parallel, a Parents’ Meeting is held, aimed at discussing topics related to parenting practices, child development, family relationships, among others. Check the calendar to find the date of the next Workshop for children and young people, as well as the next Parents’ Meeting.

Young People’s Meeting (in French and Portuguese) Meetings / activities scheduled for teenagers (14 to 18 years old), guided according to the moral teachings of spiritualist philosophy. These meetings take place on Saturdays, every other week, from 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM. In parallel, parents can participate in the Parents’ Meeting, aimed at discussing topics related to parenting practices, family relationships, among others. Check the calendar to find the date of the next meeting.
