Welcome to the CESF website!

The Spiritist Study Center Fraternity (CESF) is a spiritist center that aims to help everyone who wishes to begin the study and practice of spiritist philosophy, who wish to know the advantages that this philosophy can bring to their life, or who wish to deepen their knowledge and studies about Spiritism.

At CESF, we develop basic and in-depth study activities on Spiritism, spiritual care, balance passes for the centers of force, spiritual treatment and development, and activities for the development of spiritist mediumship.

If you seek a spiritist center to deepen your studies, share your knowledge, seek help, or even volunteer to help others, CESF is ready to welcome you genuinely, with great care and respect.

We invite you to explore our site, consult our activities, and learn more about CESF and spiritist philosophy in general.

We are sure you will enjoy a visit to our spiritist center!

Spiritism and Science

Is Spiritism a science?

By Charles Kempf

The words spiritualism and spiritualist have a very general meaning: anyone who thinks there is something different from matter in them is a spiritualist. On the other hand, the terms SPIRITISM and SPIRITIST are neologisms, that is, words invented by its codifier, Allan Kardec.

Allan Kardec defines Spiritism as “a science that deals with the nature, origin and destiny of Spirits and their relationship with the corporeal world.” Spiritism is therefore well defined as science. But it differs from scientific disciplines already established and studied in academies by the object of its studies: the spiritual element.

Spiritist Practice

All spiritualist practice is free, according to the Gospel principle: “Give freely what you have received freely”.

Spiritualist practice has no external worship, according to the Christian principle that God should be worshipped in spirit and in truth.

Spiritism has no priesthood, gurus, and the like. It does not adopt or use in its meetings and practices: facings, alcoholic beverages, incense, smoke, altars, icons, stretchers, candles, processions, talismans, amulets, sacraments, granting of indulgences, horoscopes, divination, pyramids, crystals, seashells, rituals, or any other form of external worship.

Spiritual Assistance

Spiritist centers are frequently sought by people who need help to resolve a variety of personal problems. Some people cannot overcome the loss of a loved one; others suffer and suffer from depression; many others seek to understand the meaning of their lives, are depressed and hopeless, and still others have physical problems whose causes have not been found.

CESF maintains a free spiritual assistance service to listen to these people with attention and seek to help them according to spiritist philosophy. By attempting to discover the main reasons for their problems, we end up relieving them. Many situations of this nature can be better understood by applying the spiritualist teaching that “every effect has a cause” and, therefore, when it is found, problems can be overcome.
