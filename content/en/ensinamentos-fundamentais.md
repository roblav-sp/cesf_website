---
title: "Fundamental Teachings"
---

God is the supreme intelligence, the first cause of all things. He is eternal, immutable, immaterial, unique, all-powerful, supremely just and good.

God created the universe, which includes all rational and irrational beings, animate and inanimate, material and immaterial.

Beyond the corporeal world, where incarnate Spirits who are men live, there exists a spiritual world, the home of disembodied Spirits.

In the universe, there are other worlds inhabited by beings at different levels of evolution: equal, more evolved, or less evolved than men.

All the laws of nature are divine laws, since God is their author. They encompass physical and moral laws.

Man is a Spirit embodied in a material body. The perispirit is the semi-material body that unites the Spirit to the material body.

Spirits are the intelligent beings of creation. They constitute the spiritual world, which pre-exists and survives all things.

Spirits are created simple and ignorant. They evolve intellectually and morally, passing from a lower order to a higher order, toward perfection, where they enjoy unalterable happiness.

Spirits preserve their individuality before, during, and after each incarnation.

Spirits reincarnate as many times as necessary for their own perfection.

Spirits are in constant evolution. In their multiple corporeal existences, they may remain stationary, but never regress. The speed of their intellectual and moral progress depends on their efforts to achieve perfection.

Spirits are of different orders, depending on the degree of perfection they have achieved: pure Spirits, who have achieved the highest degree of perfection; good spirits, in whom the desire for good predominates; imperfect Spirits, characterized by ignorance, desire for evil, and inferior passions.

Relations between Spirits and men are constant and have always existed. Good Spirits call us to do good, support us in life's trials, and help us bear them with courage and resignation. Wicked spirits deceive us.

Jesus is the guide and model of all Humanity. And the philosophy that he taught and practiced is the purest expression of God's Law.

The morality of Christ, contained in the Gospel, is the path to follow for the safe evolution of all men, and its practice is the solution to all human problems, as well as the goal to be achieved by Humanity.

Man has free will to act, but is responsible for the consequences of his actions.

The future life reserves for men pains and pleasures compatible with their behavior in accordance with or against God's Law.

Prayer is an act of worshipping God. It is in natural law and results from an innate feeling in man, just as the idea of the Creator's existence is innate.

Prayer makes man better. He who prays with fervor and confidence is stronger against the temptations of evil, and God sends him good Spirits to help him. It is help that is never denied him when asked with sincerity.

Source: adapted text, Brazilian Spiritist Federation - FEB
